1. Who we are and the roles involved

Celerion Practice is software that helps registered medical practitioners ("Doctors") maintain patient records, manage appointments, and prepare clinical documentation.

Under the Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 ("DPDP Act"):

  • The Doctor (or their clinic) is the Data Fiduciary for their patients' personal data. The Doctor decides what patient data is collected and why, and is the patient's first point of contact for it.
  • Celerion Health acts as a Data Processor, processing patient data only on the Doctor's behalf and instructions, to provide the app's features.
  • For the Doctor's own account data (name, registration number, clinic details, login credentials, usage records), Celerion Health is the Data Fiduciary.

2. What data the app processes

About patients (on behalf of the Doctor): name, age, sex, phone number, allergies, medical conditions, visit notes and dictation transcripts, prescriptions, vital signs, laboratory results and report images/documents, appointment and billing records, consent records, and (where the patient links one) ABHA number and address.

About Doctors: name, qualifications, medical council registration number, clinic name and address, phone, email, authentication data, and records of feature usage needed for service operation, support, and billing.

The app does not collect patient data directly from patients; all patient data is entered by, dictated by, or provided to the Doctor in the course of care.

3. Purposes of processing

Patient data is processed solely to provide the app's functions to the Doctor: maintaining the medical record; generating structured notes and prescriptions from the Doctor's dictation; running medication safety checks; reading uploaded lab reports into structured values; producing history summaries and answering the Doctor's questions about a patient's own record; sending appointment and follow-up reminders and prescriptions to the patient's phone where the patient has consented; and billing within the clinic. Celerion Health does not use patient data for advertising and does not sell personal data.

4. AI processing — plain-language disclosure

Some features send data to an artificial-intelligence service to do their work:

  • Visit dictation transcripts, together with the patient's recorded conditions and allergies, are processed by an AI model (Anthropic's Claude, via our secured server) to produce a draft note and prescription which the Doctor reviews and can edit before anything is saved.
  • Uploaded lab-report images/PDFs are processed the same way to extract test values, which the Doctor confirms before saving.
  • History summaries and record Q&A send the relevant parts of that patient's record to the AI model to generate the response.

Voice audio itself is converted to text on the Doctor's device by Apple's on-device speech recognition; audio recordings are not sent to Celerion's servers. AI outputs are drafts: the Doctor remains solely responsible for the final medical record and prescription. Our AI provider processes this data to provide the service and, under our API terms, does not use it to train its models.

5. Storage, security and location

Data is stored in a managed PostgreSQL database and object storage (Supabase), project region [REGION — e.g., Mumbai/Singapore]. Safeguards include: encryption in transit (TLS) and at rest; per-doctor row-level isolation so one Doctor's records are technically inaccessible to another; API keys and integration credentials held server-side only; and audit logging of sensitive actions. Device-level protections (passcode/Face ID) are the Doctor's responsibility.

6. Sharing

Patient data is shared only: (a) with service providers strictly needed to run the app (database/hosting, AI processing as in §4, and — when enabled — an SMS/WhatsApp delivery provider for reminders and prescriptions the Doctor sends); (b) with the patient themselves (e.g., their prescription to their phone number); (c) with India's ABDM network, only if the Doctor and patient use ABHA linking, per ABDM's consent framework; (d) where required by law. Each provider is bound to process data only for these purposes.

7. Retention and deletion

Patient records are retained while the Doctor maintains them, in line with medical record-keeping norms applicable to the Doctor. Doctors can delete records and patients within the app; deletion cascades to associated notes, prescriptions and documents. On termination of a Doctor's account, data is deleted or returned per the service agreement, subject to legal retention duties.

8. Patient rights (DPDP)

Patients may exercise DPDP rights — access, correction, erasure, grievance, withdrawal of consent for optional processing such as phone reminders — through their Doctor, who controls the record. Celerion Health supports Doctors in fulfilling these requests (e.g., consent withdrawal switches off reminders immediately; corrections are added as dated amendments preserving the original, per medico-legal norms).
